**What they show.** The original instrument's materials, as published descriptions and parts
listings give them (decisions.md R-LOOK lists the sources) and as Wikimedia Commons' "Minimoog
panel.jpg" (a 1970s Minimoog, Alex Harden, CC BY 2.0, the photograph the drawn panel was
measured from) shows them: a walnut case, an aluminium face with a textured black finish
printed in white, the knobs of the maker's modular systems (black, skirted, fluted, a spun
"mirror" aluminium cap, a white dot), wedge pointer knobs with a cream line, blue and orange
rockers, filament lamps behind jewels, metal jacks. The photograph was only looked at; no
photograph was given to the image generator, and none of the owner's own instrument was used.
No picture carries a name, a logo or lettering.

**How they were made (2026-10-09).** Each was generated by OpenAI's image model through the
Codex CLI (version 0.160.1, its `image_generation` tool) from the prompt of the same name in
`prompts/`, as Codex was given it. The round parts were asked for under a ring light, evenly
all round, so that they can turn without their light turning; the panel lays its own fixed
lamp over them. Then, with ImageMagick 7:
- each part cut out of its plain grey ground by a flood fill from the corners (7 % fuzz), its
  edge eased by a one-pixel erosion and a 0.9-pixel blur, and trimmed;
- the knobs squared about their caps' axes, not their outlines' boxes: as generated, each cap
  sat about 1 % of the knob's width above its skirt's middle (the generator's slight
  perspective), so a knob turned about its outline's middle would have wobbled its cap; turned
  about the cap's, its skirt moves under half a pixel at the plug-in's usual size. The pointer
  knob squared about its body (its wedge reaches the picture's edge);
- the knobs' light evened round them (`even.py`, the CA-74's with this panel's marks and
  edges): the generated grips leaned 18 to 30 levels brighter towards twelve o'clock than six,
  and turned, that side would have gone round with the knob. Each ring about the axis is
  divided by its own light smoothed round it and multiplied by its mean, leaving the flutes, the
  white dot and the pointer; afterwards no ring leans more than 5 levels
  (`the_knobs_pictures_are_lit_evenly_all_round`). The caps are drawn unturned over the
  turned knob (`skin::Part::KnobCap`): a spun disc's sheen lies where the lamp is however the
  knob is turned;
- quieted (`quiet.py`, 2026-10-10; the owner: "all the knobs look like they have white halos.
  and why do the knobs always have 4 shiny places that have nothing to do with the light?"):
  the ring light had left broad reflections round the skirts (four, five and six times round)
  and a bright line round each outline. Each smooth skirt's (and the pointer knob's body's)
  light is made the same all round but for what is finer than a few degrees; each fluted grip's
  to below its flutes' count (the flutes stay); the edge between grip and skirt, off the axis,
  is left as it is; the outline's band is held to a dark grey. The big knob's skirt's pattern
  four times round went from 0.34 of its light to 0.03, the knob's from 0.68 to 0.08; the panel's
  lamp lights the outline's near side (`art::lamp_over`);
- resized (Lanczos) to the sizes above; the face made grey (its colour is a black a touch warm,
  `skin::FACE_TINT`).

**The strip's two (taken 2026-10-09).** `button.png` and `display-glass.png` are the CA-74's, made
there on 2026-10-08 by the same tool from the prompts of the same names (kept in `prompts/` as
Codex was given them): the cap cut out of its grey ground as the knobs were and resized from
1254 pixels square, the glass resized from 1672 by 941. Neither shows anything particular to
that instrument; the strip's tabs, readouts and print are this panel's own (decisions.md
R-LOOK).

**ULTRA's (made 2026-10-10).** QUALITY's toggle and ULTRA's lamp and well were generated by
the same tool for the browser mock-up the owner steered them in (decisions.md R-ULTRA), from
the prompts in `prompts/` named `ultra-*` (as Codex was given them; an edit's prompt names the
picture it was given), and cut out as the others are, with these differences:
- the toggle: generated bare in the middle position (`ultra-toggle-bare`), its up and down
  positions edited from it, its middle edited to point straight out (`ultra-toggle-straight`)
  and then the slightest angle down (`ultra-toggle-slight`, the owner: "Give it the slightest
  bit of angle"), so that the four match; cut without trimming, the three cropped on one frame
  (the union of their outlines), the nut's disc made opaque (the flood fill found the grey of
  its chrome in places), and resized to 180 wide;
- the lamp: generated dark (`ultra-lamp-off-b`), its lit picture an edit of it (`ultra-lamp-on`,
  the same to the pixel but for the light); each resized to 300 and split in two at the lens's
  rim, the lens a disc of 0.80 of the radius, the bezel the rest (its hole 0.77);
- the floor: the middle 56 % of a picture of the inside seen through the panel
  (`ultra-inside`), darkened from 75 % in its middle to 30 % at its edge, cut to a disc.

**QUALITY's opening's other things (made 2026-10-10,** decisions.md R-ULTRA; the mock-up's
pictures first, the prompts in `prompts/ultra-*`):
- the Tesla lamp: an edit of the amber lamp's dark picture (`ultra-tesla-lamp-off`, the same
  bezel and framing, the lens cobalt, a coil where the bulb was), its lit picture an edit of
  that (`ultra-tesla-lamp-on`, no sparks: they are drawn); cut from their grey (the corners'
  flood fill, 7 %), cropped on one frame (1129 by 1122 at 59, 44 of 1254), resized to 300 and
  split at the lens's rim as the amber one's (the lens a disc of 120.5 pixels, the bezel outside
  116.5, each eased over a pixel and a half);
- the wheel: generated round and true (`ultra-hamster-wheel`; a first, "rickety", was out of
  true: "don't make the cage warped like that"), keyed out of its grey by its colour everywhere
  (not by a flood fill: the grey shows through it), cropped about its hub; its 31 back spokes
  made 26 by moving its own spokes, each with its joint at the rim and its head at the hub, to
  26 even places (its rims, rungs and hub kept, the hub's plate grown 14 % over the old heads'
  stubs); its back, inside the inner rim (0.87 of its half width), darkened to 0.55 of its
  light and 0.38 just inside the rim; resized to 300;
- the hamster: a strip of eight frames of a run (`ultra-hamster-run`), cut out of its grey,
  split at the gaps between the frames, lined up by the nose and laid side by side, resized to
  frames 230 wide; asleep (`ultra-hamster-asleep`), cut out.

**The name plate (made 2026-10-10;** the owner: "it's not up to the same realistic quality as
everything else"). Generated by the same tool from `prompts/plate.txt`, blank and lit from up
and to the left (it never turns); cut with a rounded rectangle (radius 9 of its 1462 by 376
pixels) where its bounds are, not by a flood fill, which found its bright chamfer's grey in
places; its ends (120 pixels each) kept and its brushed middle narrowed to the plate's 261 by
82 units (the generator's was about 3.9 to 1), and resized to four pixels a unit; then its
light dialled back (the owner: "dial back the lighting effect of the badge a bit"): every colour
scaled to 80 % (`+level 0%,80%`), its bright chamfer and its sheen with it, the black face
hardly changed. Another,
black enamel in an aluminium rim, was generated and not used: it read as a licence plate.

The generated originals, the masks, the runs' logs and the browser mock-up the owner chose from
are kept outside the repository, on the lab's share (`ca-72/look`; ULTRA's in `ultra-parts`
and `ultra-concepts`).
