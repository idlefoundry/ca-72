# SIGNAL BASIN

*A skin pack for Idle Foundry's CA-72, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
(`LICENSE.txt`).*

Weathered teal enamel with black panels let into it, edged in copper and screwed down with
bronze screws; satin cream knobs, black lamp rockers and amber lamps, in a walnut case. Its arrangement follows the concept's: the controllers' bay; a
framed central bay holding the external input and noise, three oscillator rows whose copper
lines leave through grommets into a bus, and the bus feeding the FILTER and the two contours;
then OUTPUT's and POWER's bays.

Install it by copying this folder into Idle Foundry's `Skins` folder (docs/skin-packs.md says
where) and choosing SIGNAL BASIN under LOOK in the editor's settings menu.

## How it was made (2026-10-16)

- **The look** follows a concept picture of a CA-72 panel, made by the same image model from a
  written brief (not kept in the pack).
- **The layout and the drawing** (`layout.toml`, `art/panel.svg`) were written by a script from
  one description of the panel, so that the drawing's panels and copper lines sit where the
  layout's controls are. It is kept outside the pack (a pack holds no code), in the
  CA-72's repository: docs/skin-packs/tools/signal-basin, with the scripts that cut and grade
  the pictures. The drawing embeds the pack's own screw picture.
- **The typeface** is Barlow Condensed (Medium, and SemiBold for bold) by The Barlow Project
  Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`), unchanged, from Google
  Fonts' repository.
- **Its look was checked** against the concept picture by a separate reviewing agent, round by
  round, until it judged the pack a faithful realisation of it.
- **The pictures** were generated by OpenAI's image model through the Codex CLI (0.160.1, its
  image generation tool), each from the prompt below as Codex was given it. Most were given a
  crop of the concept picture as their reference ("the attached picture") and made on a
  transparent ground; POWER's rocker, the jack and the orange rocker (unused by this layout)
  were made from words alone on a grey ground. Then each was:
  - cut out: by its own transparency, its edge eased a pixel; or out of its grey by a flood
    fill from the corners, its edge eased (a one-pixel erosion, a 0.9-pixel blur);
  - for the round parts, squared about the part's axis at the slot's framing
    (docs/skin-packs.md), the pointer knob about its round body; the jewel, jack and screw lit
    evenly all round (each ring about the axis divided by its own light smoothed round it and
    multiplied by its mean). The knobs were already lit evenly from above, and their caps turn
    with them (`knob_caps = "turn"`), their line running from their middle;
  - the black rocker's lamp moved from its raised half to its flat half, so that a switch shows
    its lamp at its "on" end when on; the raised half filled in, row by row, from the paddle on
    either side;
  - the name plate's four rivets painted out in its face's colour (the plug-in prints its
    lettering where they were);
  - graded towards the concept's colours: the wood to a dark, dull walnut about #4e3628 (its
    figure sharpened); the knobs to a satin cream about #e0d4b4 (their highlights pressed
    down); the face to a weathered petrol teal about #172a30 (its fine speckle softened, a
    blotchy patina that tiles); the plate's face rebuilt inside its rim from its own middle
    (the plug-in prints its lettering there) and its copper dulled;
  - resized (Lanczos) to its slot's size; the face's broad light made even (each pixel divided
    by the light about it, 60 pixels round) and made seamless (its half-turned copy blended in
    towards its edges) so that it tiles.
