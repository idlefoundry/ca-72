# CA-72 0.3.0 (draft)

*A draft: the notes are finished at release.*

## In this release

- **An arpeggiator.** Press **ARP** on the rail (after the keyboard key) and a drawer drops down
  over the lower panel with the arpeggiator's controls: **ON** and **LATCH**, **DIRECTION** (UP,
  DOWN, UP/DOWN, RANDOM), **RATE** (a half note to a thirty-second, with dotted and triplet values),
  **OCTAVES** (1 to 4), **GATE** and **SWING**. It plays the keys you hold in turn, in time with your
  host: locked to its bars while the transport plays, at its tempo while it is stopped. Its notes go
  through the instrument just as played ones do, so MONO, POLY, UNISON, NOTE PRIORITY, RETRIGGER and
  GLIDE all work with it. Every setting can be automated and is saved with presets and projects.
  In the VST3, CLAP and Audio Unit plug-ins and the standalone application, where **TEMPO** in
  Audio & MIDI sets its tempo (120 BPM by default).

Your presets and projects open with the arpeggiator off and sound exactly as before.
